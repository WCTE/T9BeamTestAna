\section{Ionization losses}

This documents descripts the attempt to fit the ionization losses of protons and deuterons in trigger scintillators 0 and 0.
Electron charge distributions means are used for an additional relative calibration of the 8 PMTs on both T0 and T1.

Assuming TS material of polystyrene of thickness 0.6~cm.
